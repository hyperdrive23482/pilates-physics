## § 02 · Why it matters  (Problem, escalated / the missing half)  — NEW SECTION

**Kicker:** § 02 · Why it matters

**Heading:** You read the body. But not *the load behind it.*

**Body:**
A Pilates spring does not deliver a fixed weight like a gym dumbbell. Its load builds as it stretches, so a color you call "heavy" lands differently depending on how far it travels and who is on the carriage.  



You see the effect of the spring you chose: the strain, or the ease. But that is only half of what is happening. The load itself, and how it shifts through the range, stays invisible. So you read the body, and fill in the load by feel.

**CTA button:** Get free access

> Names the internal stake (you see the effect, not the load) without crossing

> pattern interrupt.
