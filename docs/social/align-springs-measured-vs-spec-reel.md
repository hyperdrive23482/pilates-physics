## Caption

Do brand new reformer springs actually match the spec sheet? I measured a set to find out.

The lovely folks at Align sent me four new springs: yellow, blue, red and green. I hooked each one to an industrial luggage scale, zeroed it, and read the load at 4 inches and 8 inches of stretch.

Then I graphed my numbers (solid lines) against the spec from Align's website (dotted lines).

Yellow, blue and red all landed within about a pound of spec. Green ran heaviest: 1 pound over at 4 inches, 2.3 pounds over at 8.

The slope of each line is the spring rate: how many pounds each extra inch of stretch adds. Yellow, blue and red nearly match their spec slopes. Green's is a little steeper, so its gap grows the further you stretch it.

I expect heavier springs to wander a bit further from spec in pounds, so this is right in line with what I'd want from a brand new set. Overall, I'm impressed.

The lens for your teaching: the number on the spec sheet is a starting point. Every spring has its own line, and it changes with how far you stretch it.
