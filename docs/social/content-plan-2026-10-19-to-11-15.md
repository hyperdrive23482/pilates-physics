## Rules from the data

- Tie every post to an exercise they teach daily (footwork, Teaser, bridge,
  hamstring curls). Abstract physics with no exercise does 3 to 4x worse.
- Demo reels over talking heads.
- Put the workshop CTA on value posts. At most one pure promo in the last week.
- Time of day doesn't matter much. Pick a consistent window.
