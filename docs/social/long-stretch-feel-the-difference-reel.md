# Long stretch: feel the difference (reel)

Instagram reel for @kaleenc_. Planned 2026-10-22, not filmed yet. In the
Pilates Physics Social artifact as draft `long-stretch-feel-the-difference`.

The experience Kaleen gives people in person: same springs, same exercise,
change only where you stand (then the gear bar) and feel the load change.

## Shot list

Same spring setting throughout: 1.5 springs. Film from the side so the body
angle and carriage travel are visible. One press out per position is enough.

**Part 1: foot position**
1. Hook on screen. Set 1.5 springs.
2. Feet on the front half of the carriage. Press out. On screen: "Shoulders
   and abs: hard."
3. Come in. Move feet to the middle of the carriage. Press out.
4. Come in. Feet back against the shoulder rests. Press out.

**Part 2: gear bar**
5. Same 1.5 springs, feet at the shoulder rests. Press out in gear 1, then
   gear 2, then gear 3.

6. End card: "Same springs. Five different exercises."

## On-screen text

- Hook: "Same springs. Watch where my feet go."
- Labels: "Front of the carriage" / "Middle" / "Shoulder rests" / "Gear 1" /
  "Gear 2" / "Gear 3"
- Close: "Same springs. Five different exercises."

## Caption (draft)

Same springs, same exercise. So why does it feel completely different? 👀

This is my favorite way to let people feel spring physics instead of hearing about it. Long stretch on 1.5 springs. First with the feet on the front half of the carriage, then the middle, then back at the shoulder rests.

Where the feet sit changes the angle of the body, and that changes how much of your body weight the arms and abs have to manage while the springs pull the carriage home. Then I do it again in each gear position. Gearing out means the springs start less stretched, so the whole press out gets lighter.

Nothing on the spring bar moved. The exercise still changed.

Try it next time you're on a reformer and tell me which one surprised you.
