# @kaleenc_ performance, Jun 18 to Oct 14, 2026

45 feed posts, pulled from Instagram web on 2026-10-14. Data:
[ig-performance-2026-06-to-10.csv](ig-performance-2026-06-to-10.csv). Times in
the CSV are UTC; the analysis converts to Pacific.

**What the numbers are.** Views are Instagram's `content_views_count` (all
formats). "Reposts" is the public repost count, a proxy for shares only: DM
sends and saves are visible only in Insights, which needs the app or Meta
Business Suite. Topic and style tags are hand-assigned from the caption.

**Caveats.** Small samples (most groups have 2 to 7 posts). The Oct 12 and Oct
14 posts were too new to judge. Medians are used so that one viral post does not
skew a group.

**Missed in the first pass:** pinned posts were skipped. The pinned "Hills I
will die on as a mechanical engineer and Pilates instructor" carousel (Sep 21)
has 54.8k views, making it the second biggest post of the last 90 days and the
strongest point-of-view post on the account. "Love my content?" (Sep 9) has
6.4k.

## Patterns

| Cut | Finding |
|---|---|
| Format | Carousels reach further (median 13.2k vs 7.6k for reels). Reels earn more per view (3.9% vs 2.0%) |
| Style | Text carousels are the repost engine (median 14). Demo reels (10.3k) beat talking-head reels (5.8k) |
| Topic, high | Cue myths, how I teach, exercise physics (Teaser, torque), body differences (tall vs short): 16k to 62k |
| Topic, low | Remo (4.4k), tool and chart announcements (6.7k), workshop promos (8.7k, 0.7 comments per 1k views), abstract physics with no exercise (4.7k) |
| Quizzes | Good reach (15k) and comments, but the lowest likes and reposts. Good for reach, weak for sharing |
| Time of day | No reliable signal. 3 to 6pm PT looks best, but that is only 6 posts and two big hits |
| Day | Thu and Fri slightly ahead, Wed weakest. Probably confounded by what ran on each day |
| Trend | Sep and Oct medians (about 7.5k) are half of Aug (15.8k). The mix moved toward Remo, promos and abstract concepts, and fewer exercise demo reels |
| Comments | Most comments per view: testing many reformers, "I hesitate to post this", comparing springs, footwork tall vs short |
