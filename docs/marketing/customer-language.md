# Voice-of-Customer Swipe File

Real, anonymized quotes from Pilates Physics 101 and 102 students, grouped by the
five messaging pillars. When you write copy, reach for their words before
inventing your own. Their language is more credible and more specific than
anything we would write from scratch.

> **Before quoting publicly with a name, check permission.** The survey asked each
> student whether their feedback could be shared and how (with a first name,
> anonymously, or not at all). These quotes are safe to use internally and to
> paraphrase. Before attaching a name to one on a public page, confirm that
> student's `share_permission` answer in the survey data.
>
> Quotes are lightly trimmed for length and readability, with meaning preserved.
> Pull the exact original from the survey data before publishing verbatim.

## Pillar 3: Every body is different

> "Short and curvy isn't the norm for my advanced classes, and I feel abandoned in
> them at times because what works for my classmates just doesn't work for me."

> "Shorter legs may need more spring tension than longer legs because they cannot
> stretch the springs as far."

> "The more I understand about how my height and weight affect my practice, the
> more I enjoy and get from it."

> "Most of this I have known as I am a bigger girl, but I didn't know the science
> behind it, which is why I took this course."
