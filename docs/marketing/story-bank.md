# Story Bank

Raw material for the first-person stories used in newsletters and social posts.
This file holds what actually happened. [story-framework.md](story-framework.md)
shapes it into a piece.

Same relationship [customer-language.md](customer-language.md) has to
[peace-framework.md](peace-framework.md): source material here, no theory. Write
things down as they happened, in whatever shape they arrive. Polishing comes
later, and polishing too early is how a true story turns into a manufactured one.

> **Client stories are anonymized by default.** No names, no studio names, and no
> detail specific enough to identify someone (an injury plus a job plus a town
> will do it). If you ever want to attach a name, get explicit permission first,
> the same way [customer-language.md](customer-language.md) requires checking a
> student's `share_permission` before quoting them publicly.

---

## How to use this

1. **Capture, do not compose.** When something happens in the studio that made
   you think, put it in the right section below in a sentence or two. It does not
   need to be good.
2. **Note the turn.** Most usable stories have a moment where you were wrong, or
   surprised, or corrected. That moment is the Realization line. Mark it.
3. **When you need a piece,** open this file, pick one entry, and run it through
   the five lines in [story-framework.md](story-framework.md).
4. **Log what shipped.** Add a date and a channel under the entry once it runs,
   so the same story does not go out twice in a month.

**Status:** scaffold. Every section below is empty on purpose.

Prompts:

- What were you doing before, and what made you walk into the first class?
- What did you expect it to be, and what was it actually?
- Was there a moment where you decided this was a career and not a hobby?

## 2. Engineering and teaching, colliding

Prompts:

- When did the two worlds first overlap? What were you looking at?
- What did the engineering training let you see that the Pilates training did
  not teach you to see?
- Did you keep it to yourself at first? Why?

This is the section most likely to produce the brand's central story, because it
is the literal origin of "powered by physics." It is also the one most at risk of
turning into a credentials list. Keep it to moments, not qualifications.

_Not yet written._

---

## 3. The moment the physics clicked

Prompts:

- What was the specific thing you finally understood, and what were you doing
  when it happened?
- What had you believed right up until then?
- Who did you try to explain it to first, and did it work?

Entries here map directly onto the Realization line. The best ones have a
before-state that was reasonable, not stupid.

_Not yet written._

---

## 4. What I got wrong as a teacher

Prompts:

- A cue you gave for years before you understood what you were asking for.
- A client you could not help at the time, and what you would do now.
- A setting you defended because it was on the chart.

These are the highest-value entries in the file and the most uncomfortable to
write. They are what makes the Mirror line credible, because the reader is
currently doing the thing you are describing. Keep the tone honest and
unpunishing. See the Do-not list in
[voice-and-messaging.md](voice-and-messaging.md): the training was the
foundation, not a failure, and that applies to your own training too.

_Not yet written._

---

## 5. Client turnarounds

Prompts:

- Someone the standard setting did not fit, and what you changed.
- Someone who thought they were bad at Pilates and was actually just mis-loaded.
- A client who could suddenly explain their own body back to you.

Anonymize as you write, not later. Change or omit identifying details at capture
time so there is nothing to scrub before publishing.

_Not yet written._

---

## 6. Why I built the workshops

Prompts:

- What was the last straw that turned this from an interest into a course?
- Who was the first version for, and how has that changed?
- What did you assume people wanted that turned out to be wrong?

Note that the survey data in [icp-profiles.md](icp-profiles.md) can support parts
of this section. Where it does, cite the quote so the story stays grounded.

_Not yet written._

---

## 7. Small studio moments and recurring lines

Prompts:

- The question you get asked in almost every workshop.
- The thing a student says right before it clicks.
- A one-line exchange you have had more than once.

Short entries. These are not full stories, they are Mirror lines and subject
lines. Two sentences each is plenty, and volume matters more than quality here.

_Not yet written._
