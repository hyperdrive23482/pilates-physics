Hi {{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }},

As Pilates instructors, we spend a lot of time learning from other people. Workshops, trainings, mentors, Instagram. But how often do we learn from ourselves?

I mean really look back at what we taught and ask: did the choices I made actually serve my goal for that client?

Most of the time, we don't. We teach, we move on to the next client, and the session lives on in our memory and maybe a few scribbled notes.

That's why I built Remo.  Remo listens while you teach and creates the most detailed notes you've ever had about your Pilates sessions.

Once I started using Remo for my own sessions, it quickly became clear that the value wasn't in just remembering.  It was in the analysis I was able to do after offloading the remembering.  

Having detailed information at my fingertips (that didn't take a bunch of time after class to write down from memory) freed up my brain space to notice more about what I was teaching and how my clients were responding.

Take my client Rachel, a former athlete working on nailing teaser.  Every few sessions we'd notice that one leg was less stable that the other in kneeling or standing. I clocked it, but it didn't become a conscious part of the alchemy of my programming until I could see that pattern repeat across multiple sessions. Remo helped me take it from a side note to something I planned around. I got more intentional, and I made better decisions while I was teaching.

I want to share this experience with you, which is why I'm running **Program with Purpose**, a six-week program for a small founding cohort of instructors, starting December 7.

Over six weeks, you'll:

- Record your sessions with Remo, the session-recording app I built (Remo Pro is included)
- Look back across a client's history so you can pick up where you left off
- Compare what you intended to accomplish with what actually happened
- Spot your own programming patterns and decide what to do with them

It includes three live sessions with me and four tutorials, and it's built around the clients you already teach. No extra sessions required, and there's a holiday break from December 19 to January 3.

The founding cohort is $49 USD, including Remo Pro access through February 11.  That's more than a 50% savings, plus nothing renews automatically. Enrollment closes December 7.

### New on the blog: Same reformer springs, different load

Programming with purpose starts with intent. And intent matters most when the same setting lands differently on different bodies.

In my new post, I run the numbers on Teaser and Feet in Straps. Put two clients on the same red and blue springs in Feet in Straps, and one has to press their legs down while the other has to hold them up. Same springs, completely different jobs for the muscles.
