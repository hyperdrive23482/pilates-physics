# Pulley Height
**Topic:** How pulley anchor height affects rope tension and range of motion; why prioritizing ROM is usually the right call

Does pulley height matter on your reformer? I've received a few questions about this over the last few weeks. So, let's dive in. First, let's determine what you mean by matter. Your answer will determine how much you care about the height of the pulley. First, we need to acknowledge that when you anchor the pulley higher than the carriage, you're actually making the force, the rope tension, greater than the spring tension. And that's just basic trigonometry. The horizontal force in this triangle is the weight of the springs. And so then the rope tension, which goes up at an angle, is slightly greater.
