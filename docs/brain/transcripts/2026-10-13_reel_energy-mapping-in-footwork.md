# Energy Mapping in Footwork (Reel, 2026-10-13)
**Format:** Reel · **Posted:** 2026-10-13
**Topic:** Mapping where energy flows between the body, the springs and friction during footwork, and how that flow tells you which kind of muscle contraction the movement needs: concentric on the way out, eccentric on the way in.
**Key concepts:** energy transfer, energy mapping, footwork, springs, friction, carriage wheels, rails, concentric contraction, eccentric contraction, constant speed

## Spoken

There is a crazy relationship between energy transfer and muscle contraction in reformer work. In footwork, we're looking at energy transfer between the body, the springs, and the wheels. Friction on the rails. And the way the energy flows between them actually explains what kind of muscle contraction is required for that movement.

On the way out during footwork, the body is emitting energy. It's giving energy. And the springs and the wheels, friction, are absorbing that energy. Then, as we reverse the movement, halfway in, moving the carriage home, the body is absorbing energy from the springs who are emitting it. And incidentally, friction, wheels, still absorbing. No matter which direction the carriage is going, friction is absorbing energy.

The energy mapping of footwork is really interesting. On the way out, when the body is emitting energy, the muscles are concentrically contracting. And then on the way in, when the body is absorbing energy, the muscles are eccentrically contracting. Pretty cool, huh?
