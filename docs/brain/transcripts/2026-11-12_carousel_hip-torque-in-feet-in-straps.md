# Hip Torque in Feet in Straps (Carousel, 2026-11-12)
**Format:** Carousel · **Posted:** 2026-11-12
**Topic:** Works out the net hip joint torque in Feet in Straps for two 5'9" clients, one 150 lbs and one 300 lbs, on the same springs. The lighter client has to press their legs down against the springs while the heavier client has to hold their legs up against gravity, so the same spring setting produces a very different type and amount of load.
