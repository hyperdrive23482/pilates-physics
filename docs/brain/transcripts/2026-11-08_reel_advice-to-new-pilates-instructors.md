1. My advice to new and aspiring Pilates instructors 🧵
2. Memorizing is the right place to start. Leave room for the nuance that comes later.
3. Teacher training is the beginning. Learn from many experts to shape your own style.
4. It's okay to say "I don't know." Just don't stop learning.
5. Anatomy tells you what's there. Exercise science tells you how it adapts.
6. Pilates is fitness. That doesn't diminish it or exempt it from exercise science.
7. The same spring setting is not the same load for everyone.
8. Not everyone moves like the model in the manual. It's a reference. Not the only way.
9. Be skeptical of any rule that claims to be universal.
10. Appreciate Joseph Pilates' system, and question it too.
11. Most "you'll get hurt" lore doesn't hold up. Ask: is this person ready for this load?
12. Be generous with other teachers. Most bad information was handed down, not invented by them initially.
13. Sessions should build on each other. Take notes so they can.
14. Repeating a workout isn't sacrilege. It's how clients see their progress.
15. Progress with load, complexity, or volume. Know your intent before you set the springs.
16. Strength matters. So do connection, coordination, balance, and body awareness.
17. Pilates won't win every "best exercise for ____" study. It doesn't need to.
18. The bigger problem is that most people don't move enough. Pilates gets them moving. That's a win.
19. People don't need to be fixed. Help them trust movement instead of fearing it.
20. What a gift it is to create a space where people move, connect, and build confidence in their bodies.
