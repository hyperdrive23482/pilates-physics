**Topic:** Spring resistance charts are misleading without context; taller people move heavier weight in footwork

Y'all, this video is going around in the Pilates sphere and it annoys the crap out of me. Yes, you did get those numbers off of a balance body chart. That balance body chart has been around for years. You can find it in their catalog. You can find it on their website. But simplifying that chart into this video is completely misleading. And that chart actually shows the truth of the resistance which is as you extend the spring it gets heavier. So if you look at this chart here it is you will see that at 6 in it gives you a certain weight. At 12 in extension it's a different weight. At 18, it's a different weight. This is really important because if you have a tall person and a short person doing footwork, the tall person is going to move heavier weight even though they have the same springs as the short person because they're going to stretch the springs further. And that's a vital piece of information to understand if you're teaching Pilates and trying to progress someone in their strength training.
