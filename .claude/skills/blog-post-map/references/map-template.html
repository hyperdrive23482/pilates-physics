Structure only. Edit labels, summaries, order and depth here, then tell Claude the map is ready and the post prose gets rewritten to match. A thread opened by one block and never closed by a later one is a dead end.

Drag a block by the grip in the left gutter. Its sub-blocks travel with it, and so does the prose when the post is rebuilt. The arrow buttons do the same thing one position at a time, which is the way that works on a touchscreen.
